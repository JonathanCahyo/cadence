      FAQ

      Good questions.

      Does the timer follow WCA rules?

      Yes. Optional 15-second inspection with alerts at 8 and 12 seconds; going over 15 s adds +2 and over 17 s is a DNF, automatically. You can also mark +2 or DNF on any solve yourself.

      Can I use my phone like a stackmat?

      Turn on Competition mode and rotate your phone. Two hand pads appear, and the timer only arms when both hands are down — just like at a competition.

      How does the solver read my cube?

      Hold your cube with green in front and white on top, then scan each face with the camera or tap the colours in. Candence counts every colour so you know all 54 stickers are right before it solves.

      I'm a complete beginner. Is Candence for me?

      Yes. Learn starts with the beginner method and moves you forward as your Ao12 drops — from your very first solve all the way to full CFOP.

      What do Ao5 and Ao12 mean?

      “Average of 5” and “average of 12”: your latest solves with the best and worst removed, then averaged. It's how speedcubers measure real progress, because one lucky solve doesn't count.

      Which methods does it teach?

      Beginner, CFOP, Roux, ZZ and Petrus — with 41 F2L, 57 OLL, 21 PLL and 42 CMLL cases to work through.
